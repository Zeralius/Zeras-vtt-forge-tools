## Unreleased

- First version. Counts every die rolled in chat while the GM has a session running, per
  player, per die size and per face, for each session and the whole campaign.
- A window with nat 1s and nat 20s per player and a chart per die size, opened from the token
  tools or the module settings.
- Whispered and self rolls show for the GM and the player who rolled them; blind rolls for the
  GM only. Dropped dice (the lower d20 of advantage) can be left out with one checkbox.
